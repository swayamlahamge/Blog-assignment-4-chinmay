01 · PART A

Loopholes that led to the deaths

Open each case file. I have tried to separate the system gaps from the people involved.

Case file 1: Atul Subhash
  • LAWA shield that can be turned into a sword. Dowry and cruelty laws protect real victims, but the Supreme Court itself warned in December 2024 that they can be misused in matrimonial fights. Without quick screening and penalties for proven false complaints, the accused carries the stress of arrest and trial first.
  • COURTDelay and distance. Multiple cases in different states mean repeated travel, expensive lawyers and time off work. Slow justice is itself a punishment.
  • GENDERRemedies depend on who you are. The domestic-violence law covers women only. A man who is genuinely harassed has little formal protection, and stigma keeps him silent.
  • MONEYLarge demands, thin scrutiny. His brother's complaint mentions a Rs 3 crore settlement demand. Maintenance and settlement figures should be tested against proof of income.
  • CHILDNo clear rule on contact with a child. Visitation is hard to enforce, and Indian law does not name parental alienation.
  • TRUSTComplaints about court conduct. His video accused people connected with the hearings of misconduct. These are allegations, but they show the need for a fast, transparent way to question how a hearing was handled.
  • CARENo pause button. There was no compulsory counselling or mediation stage to cool the fight before it grew.
Case file 2: Ketan Agarwal (Siya-Ketan)
  • CONSENT"Yes" given out of fear is not consent. Her brother reportedly told police the family would have stopped the wedding if she had objected. Police sources claim she allegedly told investigators that calling off the marriage felt harder than the act she is accused of. Whatever the court decides, this silence is a family failure.
  • SIGNSWarnings were softened. Reports say Ketan worried about her closeness to a friend; relatives reassured him that nothing was wrong because the families were linked.
  • VETTINGThe match rested on relatives' word. The father said the families had known each other for decades, and mediators had vouched for her. Trust replaced honest, direct conversation between the couple.
  • POLICE"Accident" was the first label. The case began as an accidental death and was converted to murder after the family doubted it and more evidence emerged. Early scene checks and phone records are essential in every unnatural death.
  • PLACEA remote edge, few eyewitnesses. Investigators describe a drop of about 400 feet.
  • MEDIAOpinions before facts. Celebrities and TV panels commented while the investigation was still on, adding to the pain of both families.

Common thread: someone was in distress, nobody felt safe to speak, and the system acted only after the damage.My summary of both case files

02 · PART B

What it does to the immediate family, and parental alienation

Atul's family

His parents and brother lost him and then fought legal battles, including a Supreme Court plea for his young son, who stays with his mother. Their grief is tied to a courtroom.

Atul's son

A small child loses a father and grows up in the middle of two families in conflict.

The accused family

Arrest, jail and open online hatred before any trial. Even if a person is later cleared, the damage to name and mental health stays.

Ketan's parents

Public grief and a sense of betrayal. One report said his grandfather died weeks later. They are now living through a long trial.

Siya's family

Shock and shame. The relatives who introduced the match now face tough questions, and the siblings cope with stigma.

Parental alienation, explained simply

It is when a child is turned against a loving parent because of what the other parent says or does, usually during a bitter separation. Signs include constant criticism of the other parent, blocking calls and visits, and forcing the child to take sides.

BehaviourPossible impact
Badmouthing the other parent dailyChild feels guilty for loving that parent
Blocking visits and callsParent and grandparents slowly become strangers
Making the child "choose"Anxiety, divided loyalty, trust problems later in life
Rewriting shared historyConfused sense of identity

Fair note: it is not a formal medical diagnosis and professionals disagree on its use. Atul's claim that he was kept from his son is an allegation, not a court finding. Courts in India look at the welfare of the child.

My biggest takeaway: ethical conduct is not about being perfect. It is about acting from clarity, with the other person's wellbeing in mind as much as mine.

04 · PART D

My 30-day plan to help prevent this

Week 1: Listen

Check in with three people I have not spoken to in a while. Ask, then listen without fixing.

Week 2: Learn

Read the basics of marriage and family rights for men and women, and the idea of consent in relationships.

Week 3: Speak up

Hold a short UHV discussion with classmates. Share helplines and call out jokes about harassment or mental health.

Week 4: Build

As a computer engineering student, make a small resource page or poster for campus, and promise to share only verified news.

Need support?

In India, Tele-MANAS gives free, 24x7 mental health support on 14416. For emergencies, call 112. Talking to someone today is a strong choice.
